02 — What Race Funded Is

Two outputs.
One program.

Race Funded is a done-with-you consulting program that builds a fan membership — a season pass your most engaged followers buy into before your first race.

The membership does two things at once. It generates direct funding before the season starts. And it produces real audience data — what your fans spend money on, where they live, what brands they already buy.

That data is what a corporate sponsor actually needs before they make a decision. The membership is the first step, not the end goal.

Output 01
Direct Season Funding
Season pass revenue in the bank before round one. A paying member base that funds your season independently of any sponsorship conversation.
Output 02
Verified Audience Profile
A documented, data-backed picture of exactly who your audience is. Built from real survey responses. The kind of intelligence a sponsor needs to say yes.
Output 03
Commercial Leverage
Walking into a sponsor conversation with a verified, paying audience that matches their customer profile changes the dynamic entirely. You're no longer asking — you're offering.
03 — How It Works
Phase 0
Intelligence Gathering
We build and deploy a bespoke audience survey to your existing following. You post two videos. The survey runs for two weeks. This is how we find out exactly who your audience is before we build anything.
Phase 1
Survey Analysis
Survey results become a full audience profile — purchase behaviour, brand alignment, content preferences. From that data, we design your season pass: what's in it, what it costs, and what your audience will actually pay.
Phase 2A
Season Pass Launch
We produce all the copy, membership page, and outreach hooks from your survey data. You review, approve, and launch. This is where the direct funding starts arriving.
Phase 2B
Corporate Member Launch
A second, separate tier for businesses — built around the audience intelligence from Phase 1. This is the warm pipeline that feeds Phase 4.
Phase 4
High-Ticket Sponsors
With a verified audience profile and a live paying member base, the commercial conversation changes. You're not cold-pitching — you're showing a sponsor documented proof of access to an audience that matches their customer.
